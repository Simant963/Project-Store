2. Data we collect

  • Account information: username, email address, password hash, role, account status, and login timestamps.
  • Developer verification: legal name, phone, country, studio name, website, government-ID type and number, and the privately stored ID image.
  • App submissions: APKs, icons, screenshots, package metadata, descriptions, support details, security-scan results, versions, and review decisions.
  • Marketplace activity: downloads, saved apps, ratings, reviews, reports, notifications, and moderation history.
  • Security and operations: IP address in administrative audit records, authentication-failure data, timestamps, and technical logs.

3. Notice, purpose, and consent

Appora processes only data reasonably necessary for the purposes stated here: creating and securing accounts; providing downloads and marketplace features; verifying developers; scanning and reviewing apps; handling reports, support, and grievances; sending service and security notices; preventing abuse; keeping required records; and complying with lawful requests. Where consent applies, it must be specific and informed and may be withdrawn as easily as it was given. Withdrawal does not invalidate prior lawful processing, but optional processing will stop.

4. Sharing and processors

Appora does not sell personal data. Data may be handled by infrastructure, database, storage, email, malware-scanning, logging, backup, and professional-service providers acting for Appora; by authorities when legally required; or during a properly safeguarded business transfer. Public profiles, app listings, ratings, and review usernames are visible as described by the interface. Government-ID documents are restricted to authorized reviewers.

5. Security and international processing

Appora uses password hashing, access controls, CSRF protection, HTTPS in production, private file routes, malware scanning, audit logs, and backups. No system is perfectly secure. Providers may process data in other locations subject to contracts and applicable transfer requirements.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request a summary of your data and processing, identities or categories of recipients, correction, completion, updating, erasure, withdrawal of consent, grievance resolution, or nomination of another person. Contact {{ config.PRIVACY_EMAIL }} with enough information to verify the account and request. The grievance process explains escalation. You may also use any regulator, board, court, or consumer remedy available by law.

7. Children

Appora accounts and developer publishing are restricted to persons aged 18 or older. Appora does not knowingly process a child’s account data or use tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. If a child’s data was submitted, contact us immediately so access can be stopped and the data reviewed for deletion.

8. Breaches, retention, and changes

Appora assesses security incidents and provides notices to affected persons and authorities where required. Retention periods are in the Data Retention Policy. Material changes receive a new effective version and, where required, notice or renewed consent.
