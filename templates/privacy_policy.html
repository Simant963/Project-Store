1. Who controls your data

2. Data we collect

  • Account information: username, email address, password hash, role, account status, and login timestamps.
  • Developer verification: legal name, phone, country, studio name, website, government-ID type and number, and the privately stored ID image.
  • App submissions: APKs, icons, screenshots, package metadata, descriptions, support details, security-scan results, versions, and review decisions.
  • Marketplace activity: downloads, saved apps, ratings, reviews, reports, notifications, and moderation history.
  • Security and operations: IP address in administrative audit records, authentication-failure data, timestamps, and technical logs.

3. Why we use it

  • To create accounts, authenticate users, deliver downloads, and operate marketplace features.
  • To verify developers, scan APKs, review submissions, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and maintain audit records.
  • To send password resets, approval decisions, security notices, and service communications.
  • To comply with lawful requests, enforce policies, resolve disputes, and protect users and the platform.

Where consent is the applicable basis, it may be withdrawn, although processing already lawfully completed is unaffected. Some information remains necessary to provide an account or meet legal and security obligations.

4. Sharing and processors

Appora does not sell personal data. Data may be handled by infrastructure, database, storage, email, malware-scanning, logging, backup, and professional-service providers acting for Appora; by authorities when legally required; or during a properly safeguarded business transfer. Public profiles, app listings, ratings, and review usernames are visible as described by the interface. Government-ID documents are restricted to authorized reviewers.

5. Security and international processing

Appora uses password hashing, access controls, CSRF protection, HTTPS in production, private file routes, malware scanning, audit logs, and backups. No system is perfectly secure. Providers may process data in other locations subject to contracts and applicable transfer requirements.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, completion, deletion, information about processing, withdrawal of consent, or grievance resolution. Send enough information to verify the account and request to {{ config.PRIVACY_EMAIL }}. You may nominate another person where applicable law provides that right. You may also appeal through an available regulator or statutory grievance mechanism.

7. Children

Appora is not intended for children to create accounts or publish apps without any consent and safeguards required by applicable law. If you believe a child provided data improperly, contact us promptly.

8. Retention and changes

Retention is described in the Data Retention Policy. Material policy changes will be identified by a new effective version and, where required, communicated or presented for renewed consent.
