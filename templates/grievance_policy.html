2. Response process

Appora records each complete grievance, acknowledges it within 24 hours, and aims to resolve it within 7 days, subject to any shorter mandatory period. Requests involving intimate imagery, impersonation, child safety, court or government orders, or immediate security risk are escalated promptly. Appora may request information needed to verify identity, authority, or the complaint.

3. Decisions and appeals

A response may explain the action taken, information needed, or reason no action was taken. If you are dissatisfied with a decision covered by India’s intermediary rules, you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee within the applicable period. Nothing here limits a court, regulator, consumer commission, law-enforcement, or other remedy available by law.

4. Privacy complaints

For access, correction, completion, erasure, consent withdrawal, or another personal-data request, email {{ config.PRIVACY_EMAIL }}. We verify requests proportionately and provide reasons where a request cannot lawfully be completed.

5. Abuse of process

Knowingly false, fraudulent, threatening, or repetitive complaints may be restricted, but good-faith reporting will not be penalized. Appora preserves evidence when required for safety, disputes, or lawful process.
