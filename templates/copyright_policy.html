Submitting a notice

Send a signed notice to {{ config.GRIEVANCE_EMAIL }} containing: your full name and contact details; identification of the protected work; the exact Appora listing or material complained of; an explanation of your rights and the alleged infringement; any registration or authorization evidence; a good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized; a statement that the information is accurate; and your physical or electronic signature.

What Appora does

Appora may acknowledge the notice, request missing information, preserve relevant records, restrict access while investigating, forward the notice to the developer, and remove or restore material based on law, evidence, exceptions, licenses, and any valid response. Incomplete or fraudulent notices may be rejected.

Developer response

A developer may identify the removed material, explain ownership, license, authorization, exception, or mistaken identification, provide contact details and supporting evidence, and sign the response. Appora may share the response with the complainant and may require a court order or other lawful process before restoration where the dispute cannot be resolved safely.

Repeat infringement

Appora may restrict or terminate developers who repeatedly or seriously infringe rights, considering valid notices, responses, court decisions, surrounding circumstances, and attempts to evade enforcement.

No legal adjudication

Appora’s review is an operational safety process and does not determine ownership as a court would. Parties remain responsible for obtaining legal advice and pursuing available remedies. False claims may create legal liability.
