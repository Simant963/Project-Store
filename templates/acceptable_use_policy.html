Protect people and devices

Do not upload or distribute malware, spyware, ransomware, credential theft, hidden miners, destructive code, unauthorized surveillance, deceptive installers, security bypasses, or code designed to evade scanning or administrator review.

Respect privacy

Apps must request only justified permissions, provide meaningful notice and choice, secure personal data, and honor deletion and withdrawal requests. Covert recording, stalking, non-consensual intimate content, doxxing, identity theft, and sale or misuse of sensitive data are prohibited.

Be honest

Do not impersonate another person or organization, misrepresent functionality, fabricate reviews or downloads, conceal fees, use misleading metadata, manipulate rankings, submit counterfeit apps, or make unsupported safety, financial, medical, or government claims.

Respect rights and safety

  • No copyright, trademark, patent, trade-secret, publicity, or other rights infringement.
  • No child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation, trafficking, credible threats, terrorism support, or instruction intended to facilitate serious wrongdoing.
  • No unlawful discrimination, targeted harassment, hateful abuse, or content illegal where Appora operates or distributes it.
  • No unlicensed gambling, prohibited financial schemes, illegal goods, or regulated services without all required authorization and safeguards.

Protect the platform

Do not probe or bypass access controls, overload services, scrape contrary to technical restrictions, abuse APIs, distribute another user’s private information, compromise accounts, or interfere with logs, scans, moderation, or availability.

Enforcement and reporting

Appora considers context, severity, history, intent, user harm, and legal duties. Responses may include a warning, rejection, restricted visibility, blocked downloads, account suspension, evidence preservation, removal, or referral to authorities. Report concerns through an app’s report control or {{ config.GRIEVANCE_EMAIL }}.
