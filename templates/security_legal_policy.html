1. Report a security issue

Email {{ config.SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with the affected URL or feature, reproduction steps, impact, and safe supporting evidence. Do not access other people’s data, disrupt service, extort, publicly expose an unresolved vulnerability, or upload live malware outside the normal isolated app-submission flow.

2. Coordinated disclosure

Good-faith researchers who stay within these boundaries should allow a reasonable remediation period. Appora will acknowledge reports, assess severity, preserve relevant evidence, and communicate material status changes when practical. This statement does not authorize activity prohibited by law or create a reward entitlement.

3. Security incidents

Appora maintains incident triage, containment, recovery, evidence preservation, and notification procedures. Reportable cyber incidents are escalated to CERT-In and affected persons or authorities within applicable statutory periods. System clocks, security logs, access controls, backups, malware scanning, and response records must be maintained under the production security runbook.

4. Government and legal requests

Requests should identify the issuing authority, legal basis, records sought, account or app identifiers, applicable period, and official contact details. Appora verifies authority and scope, discloses no more than legally required, preserves confidentiality where required, and may notify affected persons unless prohibited or unsafe. Emergency requests must describe the imminent risk and why ordinary process is inadequate.

5. User safety

For immediate danger contact local emergency services. Indian cybercrime and financial-fraud reports may be made at cybercrime.gov.in or helpline 1930. Appora support is not an emergency service.
