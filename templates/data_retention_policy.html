Active accounts and listings

Appora retains account, verification, app, security, download, review, report, notification, and audit information while it is needed to operate the service, meet user expectations, protect the marketplace, enforce agreements, and comply with law.

Soft deletion and Trash

Normal administrator deletion is recoverable. The record is marked deleted and access or public distribution stops, but related database records and private files remain available to authorized administrators for restoration, appeals, security investigation, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

Permanent deletion

Only the main administrator can permanently delete a record, and must re-enter the exact item name and administrator password. Permanent deletion removes the primary database record and associated stored files from the active system. Copies may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until those backups rotate, or longer when preservation is legally required.

Operational retention schedule

  • Authentication throttling records: only as long as needed to enforce the active security window and investigate abuse.
  • Password-reset tokens: retained for security auditing after expiry or use, then removed under the operator’s maintenance schedule.
  • Administrative audit records and security findings: retained for the period established by the operator’s legal and security review.
  • Backups: retained according to the documented production backup rotation and restored only for continuity or investigation.
  • Government-ID material: retained while developer verification, distribution, disputes, or legal duties require it, then securely deleted.

Before launch, {{ config.LEGAL_OPERATOR_NAME }} must replace these purpose-based periods with counsel-approved numeric retention periods matched to its backup automation and legal obligations.

Deletion requests and legal holds

Request deletion at {{ config.PRIVACY_EMAIL }}. Appora may verify identity and may retain limited information when necessary for fraud prevention, security, rights claims, accounting, enforcement, or legal process. When a hold ends, information is returned to the normal deletion schedule.
