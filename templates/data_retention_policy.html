1. Retention principles

Appora keeps personal data only for a stated service, security, dispute, or legal purpose. A legal hold, active investigation, court order, statutory duty, fraud risk, or unresolved rights claim may extend a period below. When the reason ends, data returns to the normal deletion schedule.

2. Standard schedule

  • Active account and app records: for the account or listing lifetime.
  • Soft-deleted accounts and listings: 30 days in Trash, then eligible for permanent deletion unless restored or held.
  • Expired or used password-reset tokens: 30 days after expiry or use.
  • Authentication throttling records: 30 days after the last recorded attempt.
  • Developer identity and verification material: while distribution access is active, then 180 days after final closure or rejection unless a dispute or law requires longer.
  • App packages and scan reports: while distributed, then 180 days after final removal for security, appeal, and malware investigation.
  • Reviews, reports, moderation decisions, and administrative audit records: 3 years after the related event or case closes.
  • Security and infrastructure logs: at least 180 days, stored within India where legally required.
  • Encrypted production backups: 30-day rolling rotation; deleted data may remain inaccessible in a backup until that backup expires.

3. Soft and permanent deletion

Normal administrator deletion is recoverable during the Trash period. Only the main administrator may use permanent deletion after a confirmation step. Permanent deletion removes the primary record and associated active-system files; it does not override a lawful hold or immediately rewrite immutable backups.

4. Identity documents

Access to government-ID material is limited to authorized verification reviewers. Appora does not request or accept Aadhaar as developer verification unless the operator first completes every applicable UIDAI registration, consent, masking, verification, storage, and security requirement. Developers should use another offered ID and must redact unrelated information where lawful.

5. Requests

Request access, correction, or deletion at {{ config.PRIVACY_EMAIL }}. Appora may verify identity and will explain any lawful retention exception. Approved requests are applied to active systems without undue delay and to backups through normal rotation.
